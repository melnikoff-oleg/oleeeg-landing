## 3. The strategy

Three moves, in this order.

**Move 1. Do not break the YouTube path.** 84% of traffic arrives from a video description
having already watched the video. The above-the-fold `RepoCta` that hands over the resource stays
exactly where it is on every page. Nothing in this pass is allowed to push it down.

**Move 2. Make every page answer the question without the video.** This is the stated goal, and it
is also the ranking fix. Today `/claude-cowork-outreach` is roughly 600 words of advice like
"make sure your LinkedIn profile is optimized", while the video it accompanies contains the actual
system: the Apollo scraper on Apify at $1.50 per 1,000 leads, the $5 of monthly free credit that
makes 3,000 leads free, the Chrome-only extension requirement, the Settings -> Connectors -> Apify
MCP install path, and the "slightly awkward compliment" tactic that beats AI-slop pattern matching.
None of that is on the page. All of it is in the transcript. The written guide goes on the page.

(The page currently states the Apify free tier is 5,000 leads a month. Oleg corrects that number
on camera: it is 3,000. The live page is repeating a mistake the video already retracted.)

**Move 3. Build the pages for keywords that exist.** New URLs, each aimed at a cluster above,
each inside real expertise. No page gets built for a term Oleg cannot answer better than the
current top result, because a thin page on a competitive term costs reputation and earns nothing.
